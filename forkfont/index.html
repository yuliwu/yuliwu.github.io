Forkfont

Typefaces and cutlery are used every day, yet rarely given the attention they deserve. Forkfont pairs twenty open-source typefaces with twenty cutlery sets still made today, matched by character: the same era, the same gesture or the same temperament. Fine words deserve exquisite type, just as delicacies deserve radiant silverware. The ink is ready, and the table is laid. Bon appétit!
